Controls (Windows / Mac):
  • Ctrl (or Cmd ⌘) + Wheel — zoom in/out (or 2-finger Pinch on Mac trackpad).
  • Normal Wheel (when zoomed) — smoothly pan up/down.
  • Shift + Wheel — pan left/right.
  • Left-click Drag — free pan anywhere.
  • Double-click / Esc — reset zoom to 100%.
