# Privacy Policy for Media Zoom Anywhere

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

Media Zoom Anywhere ("the Extension") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Media Zoom Anywhere does **not** collect, store, track, monitor, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, website content, keystrokes, IP addresses, or analytical data. 

## 2. Local Operation Only
All operations performed by the Extension (zooming, panning, and adjusting visual styling of images, videos, and canvas elements) occur entirely within your local browser environment. The Extension does not communicate with any external servers, third-party analytics services, or advertising networks.

## 3. Permissions Used
The Extension requests the minimum permissions necessary to deliver its core functionality:
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your personal user preferences (such as zoom speed, activation modifier keys, scrollbar hiding preference, and custom keyboard zoom shortcuts) locally on your device or via standard browser synchronization (`chrome.storage.sync`).
- **`<all_urls>` / Host Access**: Required solely to inject the zoom script (`content.js`) across web pages so that you can inspect and zoom media on websites you visit. No data from these pages is ever read, recorded, or transmitted.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
